Frequently Asked Questions

What's the deposit requirement?

$75 non-refundable deposit per artist is required to reserve your event. This will be deducted from your total on the day of service.

When is payment due?

The balance is due in full on the day of service. We accept cash or prepayment via Visa/Mastercard. We do not accept checks.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancellations must be made within 30 days of your scheduled appointment. After that time, you're responsible for the cost of all scheduled services.

Can I change the number of services?

Yes! All changes must be made 60 days prior to your event.

Do you offer trial services?

Yes! Trial runs are available so you can see exactly how your look will appear on the day of your event.

What about gratuity?

Gratuity is appreciated but not included in pricing. Our satisfied clients typically give 15-20% as a token of appreciation.
